🎮 Dots and Boxes - Complete Game Rules

Welcome to Dots and Boxes! A strategic game that enhances your thinking capability and focus.

🎯 Game Modes

  • Single Player: Play against an intelligent AI opponent with strategic gameplay
  • Two Players: Play with a friend on the same device with local multiplayer
  • Online Multiplayer: Play with friends online using lobby codes with real-time synchronization

🎲 Core Gameplay

  • Players take turns drawing horizontal or vertical lines between adjacent dots
  • Complete the fourth side of a square to claim it and earn 1 point
  • Completing a square does NOT grant an extra turn
  • The game ends when all possible lines are drawn
  • Player with the most completed squares wins!

🎲 Dice System

  • Roll the dice at the start of your turn to determine how many lines you can draw
  • Dice values 1-5: Draw that many lines during your turn
  • Dice value 6: Triggers special effects based on game mode:
    • Single Player & Two Players: Lucky Draw wheel with random effects
    • Online Multiplayer: Lucky Draw wheel is disabled for balanced gameplay

🎡 Lucky Draw Wheel (Single Player & Two Players Only)

  • Trigger: Activated when you roll a 6 on the dice
  • Wheel Effects:
    • "🎯 Extra Turn" - Get an additional turn immediately
    • "🎲 Roll Again" - Get an extra dice roll after finishing current lines
    • "💨 Poof! Turn vanished" - Your turn ends immediately, skip to opponent
    • "⭐ Bonus Points" - Earn bonus points for completed squares
    • "🔄 Switch Turns" - Immediately switch to opponent's turn
  • Important: Lucky Wheel can only be triggered once per turn
  • Note: Lucky Wheel is disabled in Online Multiplayer mode for fair gameplay

🏆 Winning Conditions

  • Normal Win: Player with most squares when all lines are drawn
  • Early Win: Player who completes more than 50% of total squares wins immediately
  • Tie Game: If scores are equal when game ends

🎨 Game Features

  • Smooth Line Drawing: Click and drag to draw lines smoothly with visual feedback
  • Player Symbols: Completed squares show X (Player 1) or O (Player 2)
  • Score Tracking: Real-time score updates for both players
  • Turn Indicators: Clear display of current player's turn and remaining lines
  • Dice Animation: Beautiful SVG dice with smooth roll animations
  • Responsive Design: Works perfectly on desktop and mobile devices
  • Audio Effects: Dice roll sound effects for enhanced experience

🌐 Online Multiplayer

  • Create Lobby: Generate a unique 6-character lobby code
  • Join Lobby: Enter a friend's lobby code to join their game
  • Real-time Sync: Game state synchronizes between players instantly
  • Player Roles: Automatic assignment of Player 1 (X) and Player 2 (O)
  • Turn Management: Only the current player can make moves
  • Connection Handling: Graceful handling of disconnections and reconnections
  • Balanced Gameplay: Lucky Wheel disabled for fair online competition

🔐 User Authentication

  • Guest Mode: Play immediately without registration
  • User Registration: Create an account to save progress and statistics
  • User Login: Sign in to access your profile and game history
  • Profile Management: View statistics, change avatar, and manage account
  • Session Management: Secure session handling with automatic logout

🎯 Strategy Tips

  • Try to complete squares while preventing your opponent from doing the same
  • Plan ahead and think about which lines will benefit you most
  • Watch your opponent's moves to understand their strategy
  • In Single Player mode, observe the AI's patterns to improve your gameplay
  • In Online mode, focus on pure strategy since Lucky Wheel is disabled
  • Use the dice system strategically - sometimes fewer lines can be more beneficial

📱 Controls

  • Mouse: Click and drag to draw lines
  • Touch: Tap and drag on mobile devices
  • Keyboard: Use arrow keys for navigation (where applicable)
  • Buttons: Click buttons for dice rolling and game actions
