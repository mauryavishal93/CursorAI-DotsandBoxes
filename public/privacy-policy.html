This Privacy Policy explains how Dots & Boxes ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the app. By using the app, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

1. Scope of this policy

This policy applies to the Android app and any related services used to provide online multiplayer gameplay, authentication, game statistics, leaderboards, and in-lobby chat.

2. Information we collect

A) Account information (if you register/login)

We may collect:

  • Username
  • Email address
  • Password (stored securely on the server)
  • Avatar (if you set one)
  • Account timestamps (e.g., created date, last login)
  • Game statistics (points, wins/losses, streaks, and score history)

If you log in as a guest, we may still create a guest account server-side to support online play.

B) Session and authentication data

We process authentication/session identifiers managed by the backend (e.g., session cookies/tokens) to support login and protect authenticated endpoints.

C) Multiplayer game data

For online multiplayer matches, we may collect and process:

  • Lobby code and your player role (player 1 / player 2)
  • Game moves (e.g., lines you draw), scores, and game results
  • Timing/turn flow information needed to enforce turn rules

D) Chat data (if you use chat)

Our server relays chat messages inside a lobby. Depending on the client implementation, chat may be sent as either plaintext (legacy/compatibility) or encrypted messages (E2EE). For encrypted messages, the server relays encrypted payload fields (e.g., ciphertext and initialization vector), and related metadata (e.g., lobby code, username, timestamp).

E) Technical and connection data

We may receive connection-related metadata from the server logs and realtime infrastructure, such as IP address, timestamps, and socket connection identifiers.

3. How we use information

We use information to:

  • Provide and run online multiplayer gameplay
  • Create/join/recover multiplayer lobbies
  • Update scores, wins/losses, and other gameplay statistics
  • Generate and display leaderboards and match history
  • Enable chat inside lobbies
  • Maintain security, reliability, and abuse prevention

4. When we share information

We share information with service providers that help us operate the game (e.g., hosting and database providers). For encrypted chat, we do not decrypt message contents on the server when encryption is enabled.

5. Data retention

We retain personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, or as required by law. Account and game statistics may persist across games.

6. Data security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information. No system is 100% secure.

7. Your choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights such as access, correction, deletion, and objection to processing. Contact us using the email listed in this policy.

8. Children's privacy

Dots & Boxes is intended for general audiences and is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in jurisdictions where applicable). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Effective date" at the top will reflect the latest updates.
